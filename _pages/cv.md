    <h2>About</h2>
    <p>
      I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science with an interdisciplinary background spanning robotics, machine
      learning, and electrical engineering. My research focuses on intrinsic robot learning in multimodal,
      contact-rich environments, leveraging information-theoretic control and hybrid dynamical systems to help
      robots acquire complex behaviors and adapt robustly to the real world — advancing the foundations of
      self-motivated, perceptually-aware robotic intelligence.
    </p>
  </section>
